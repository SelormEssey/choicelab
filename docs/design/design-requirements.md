# Design requirements

## Status

These are preliminary design directions derived from the project framing. They are not validated user requirements and must be revised after formative research.

## Provisional requirements

| ID | Direction | Rationale to investigate | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DR-01 | Make material assumptions visible. | People may need to assess what an output depends on. | Formative accounts and literature. |
| DR-02 | Make tradeoffs legible across options. | A decision may involve consequences that a single recommendation obscures. | Observation of decision strategies and comprehension needs. |
| DR-03 | Preserve meaningful user control. | The person remains responsible for a financial decision. | Evidence about agency, preference, and control. |
| DR-04 | Communicate uncertainty without false precision. | Financial support can be misread as authoritative. | Evidence about interpretation and calibrated trust. |
| DR-05 | Support accessible, low-burden interaction. | Decision support should not depend on visual, motor, or cognitive exclusion. | Accessibility review and usability evaluation. |

## Traceability rule

Each future design requirement should link to one or more sources: formative research, prior literature, accessibility guidance, or a documented technical constraint. If a requirement is a hypothesis, label it as such.
