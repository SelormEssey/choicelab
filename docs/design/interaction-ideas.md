# Interaction ideas

## Purpose

This document records early interaction directions for exploration. These ideas are not completed designs, user-tested patterns, or product commitments.

## Reflection-first direction

A reflection-first flow may invite a person to identify what matters in a decision, examine a tradeoff, compare possible consequences, and make a decision with that context visible.

Possible prompts to explore:

- What outcome matters most in this situation?
- Which tradeoff feels most difficult?
- What would change your choice?
- What information is still uncertain?

## Recommendation-first comparison direction

For a future comparative study, a recommendation-first condition may foreground a proposed option and then provide supporting information. The comparison must be designed carefully so differences in content, effort, and clarity do not confound the interaction order.

## Design questions

- How can a prompt support reflection without implying a correct answer?
- Which assumptions and consequences should be visible at each moment?
- How can the interface support a person who wants to move quickly while preserving meaningful choice?
- What will count as a fair, understandable comparison condition?
