# Evaluation plan

## Status

No evaluation has been conducted. This document describes a planning direction that will be revised after formative research, prototype definition, and appropriate review.

## Intended comparison

ChoiceLab currently implements a four-condition comparison of scenario-only, recommendation, explanation, and confidence-cue interfaces using fictional resource-selection scenarios. The implementation is a research prototype, and no evaluation has been conducted.

## Candidate outcomes

The primary provisional outcomes are:

- understanding of assumptions, options, and consequences
- perceived agency in the decision process
- calibrated trust in the interface support

Additional outcomes may include usability, decision confidence, completion time, and qualitative accounts of the experience. These will be included only if they serve the refined research question.

## Study design considerations

- Choose between-subjects or within-subjects design after considering learning and carryover effects.
- Keep scenario information equivalent across conditions unless a documented manipulation requires a difference.
- Predefine inclusion, exclusion, and analysis decisions where appropriate.
- Combine quantitative measures with qualitative evidence when this helps explain experience.
- Report null, mixed, and negative results with the same care as positive results.

## Reporting commitments

Any future report will distinguish between observed findings, interpretation, and limitations. It will describe participants, methods, measures, analysis, and deviations from plan without exposing identifiable information.
