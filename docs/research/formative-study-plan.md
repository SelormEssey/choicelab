# Formative study plan

## Purpose

The formative study will help ChoiceLab understand how people approach bounded decision-support tasks and what they need from decision-aid interfaces. It is separate from the current controlled core study and is not intended to test effectiveness claims before an appropriate protocol exists.

## Study status

No participants have been recruited, no sessions have been conducted, and no findings are available at this stage.

## Proposed method

The exact method will be finalized after literature review, ethics review, and feasibility assessment. A likely approach is a semi-structured interview or contextual inquiry focused on participants' existing decision strategies, information needs, and perceptions of assistance.

## Questions under consideration

- How do participants describe the steps and uncertainties in the chosen decision context?
- Which tradeoffs do they consider important?
- What sources or tools do they use, and why?
- What makes an interface feel supportive, controlling, transparent, or difficult to use?

## Planning requirements

- Define eligibility and recruitment criteria without overclaiming representativeness.
- Prepare informed consent and a withdrawal process appropriate to institutional requirements.
- Avoid requesting credentials, personal records, or unnecessary sensitive details.
- Create a data management plan before collection.
- Determine whether institutional ethics review or faculty approval is required.
