# Research questions

## Current prototype question

> How do AI recommendations, explanations, and confidence cues affect human decision-making and reliance?

This question guides the current research prototype. It remains open to refinement through documented formative work and evaluation.

## Construct definitions to refine

| Construct                       | Working meaning                                                                                    | Open question                                                         |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Understanding                   | A participant's grasp of relevant assumptions, options, and consequences.                          | What evidence should demonstrate understanding in the chosen context? |
| Perceived agency                | A participant's sense that they can meaningfully direct and own the decision.                      | How can this be distinguished from confidence or task control?        |
| Trust                           | A participant's calibrated willingness to rely on interface support.                               | Which dimensions of trust matter in the chosen decision context?      |
| Reflection-first assistance     | An interaction direction that foregrounds questions, tradeoffs, and consequences before an answer. | Which interaction elements represent this direction faithfully?       |
| Recommendation-first assistance | An interaction direction that foregrounds a proposed answer before deeper reflection.              | What constitutes a fair comparison condition?                         |

## Candidate secondary questions

These are prompts for later refinement, not committed hypotheses.

- How do people interpret the confidence and authority implied by a decision-aid interface?
- What forms of explanation or comparison support deliberation without creating unnecessary burden?
- How do time pressure, prior knowledge, and perceived stakes shape preferences for assistance?
